Authentic serenity in Khénifra's cedar forest

Hidden among thousand-year-old cedars in the Middle Atlas, Palais de Cèdre is an intimate haven where Berber soul meets contemporary refinement. This boutique luxury hotel in Khénifra, Morocco, offers an escape into nature without sacrificing elegance.

Sustainably designed with local stone and artisanal touches, our hotel offers a sanctuary for the senses: authentic Moroccan cuisine, a traditional hammam, and starry nights by the fireplace. Reconnect with the cedar forest – one of Morocco's most treasured landscapes.

Every detail whispers tranquility — from the murmur of the nearby river to the golden light filtering through cedar branches. Here, time slows down, and luxury is measured in moments of genuine peace.
